# Wildmon walkthrough narration

## 00:00:00 — A closer look

Step out. Look closer. Wildmon turns a walk outside into a nature discovery game, one real observation at a time.

## 00:00:09 — Capture → discover

Start with a quick capture. Choose a clear photo of one plant, bird, or insect, then submit it. Identification runs in the background, so you can put your phone away. Here's an earlier, genuinely processed discovery. Verified results join your collection and earn experience points; uncertainty stays visible.

## 00:00:32 — Make time for outside

Want a little more structure? Pick a ten, twenty, or thirty minute hunt. Start the timer, explore, and finish when you're ready. Your summary brings the session's discoveries and rewards together.

## 00:00:46 — Explore together

Friend challenges give two explorers the same targets. Choose supported categories or visual traits. Both players must be ready before the server starts one shared clock. Verified evidence decides the result.

## 00:01:00 — A little more, every day

Ecosystem packs turn discoveries into a lasting checklist. Progress carries across days, and earlier verified observations can count when you join. Each photograph fills one matching slot in a pack.

## 00:01:14 — Progress with a purpose

Earn badges, build your collection, and compare server-awarded experience on weekly or all-time leaderboards. The reward is noticing more of the living world.

## 00:01:25 — Behind the discovery

Behind the scenes, Next.js connects to a FastAPI backend. MongoDB Atlas stores discoveries and durable processing jobs. A separate Python worker asks Gemma to examine each image. Pydantic validates the response, and server rules award rewards once. Processing continues after you leave the page.

## 00:01:47 — Find more outside

A quick photo. A shared adventure. A growing connection with nature. Wildmon. Find more outside.
